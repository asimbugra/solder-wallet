Backup words

These words are the wallet. Write them on paper in this order and keep it somewhere safe. Anyone with them can take your funds.

    The words are on the device screen, they never travel over WiFi.

    Check your paper backup

    Find each numbered word on your paper. On Solder, tap BOOT to change the selected word, then hold BOOT for 1.5 seconds to choose it.

    Check your copy

    Type the words at these positions from your paper.

    Restore

    Enter your 12 or 24 backup words, separated by spaces. Phantom and Solflare words work here.

    Auto lock

    Lock the WiFi first

    Choose a new password to replace the device’s setup password. Solder restarts to use it.

    8 characters or more. You will type it once on your phone after the restart.

    Restarting

    1. Solder restarts with the new password.
    2. Your phone drops the WiFi. Join Solder again with the new password.
    3. This page continues by itself.
